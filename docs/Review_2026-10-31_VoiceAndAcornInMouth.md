# Luigi's chat, 31 Oct: voice and Acorn in Mouth (to go over later)

A Discord chat between the user (4iZard) and Luigi after Luigi played a build on 31 Oct. Not acted on yet: it's for a design session after the current work (the 31 Oct review fixes: no timer, the car flipping) is done.

## What Luigi said
- **Voice (VOIP) was better** than last time. **No crashes.**
- **Acorn in Mouth doesn't work in practice.** There was never a need to talk beyond reacting to what happened, so losing your voice felt like an annoyance, not a challenge. Luigi's guess: "99% of players would end up just switching over to discord and disabling voice chat altogether."
- **Why talking isn't needed:** for voice to matter in play, players need to know different things. "There is nothing the pedaler knows that the steerer does not know, so communication is a bit redundant."
- **Where talking IS needed:** hard manoeuvres. You have to go slow, and before the pedal squirrel reverses, the wheel squirrel has to be ready to steer, "otherwise we go off a cliff."
- Taking voice away is still not a good challenge, in Luigi's view.

## The user's ideas from the chat
- Instead of muting, make the squirrel's voice **muffled** (talking through an acorn). Luigi: worth trying, "just for fun".
- **Sections that need much more talking.**
- Back to the idea board for **giving each player different information**.

## Done since
- **Muffle instead of mute** (the user, 31 Oct: "lets try muffle for the next test"): built, checked in PIE only. How to check it on 2 PCs is in [Playtest.md](Playtest.md#muffled-voice-31-oct-check-by-hand).
- **When** (31 Oct): there's no test before friend test 3 (28 Nov), so the muffle gets its first test there, and the design session below comes after it ([Roadmap](Roadmap.md#plan-to-friend-test-3-decided-18-oct-replanned-31-oct)).

## Questions for the design session
1. After the next test: keep the muffle, make it rarer, or drop Acorn in Mouth?
2. **Asymmetric information**, so the crew has to talk. Already in the backlog: [Roadmap #9](Roadmap.md), the pedal squirrel can't see the road. Other seeds to weigh:
   - Only the WHEEL squirrel sees the road well (the seat sits low or behind the windscreen junk); a rider seat sees further ahead and calls the turns.
   - Only the PEDALS squirrel sees the gauges (RPM, damage); the others can't.
   - Only a squirrel near a part sees how badly it's broken or which repair it needs.
   - The route's hazards (the dog, the wrecking ball's swing, a cliff edge) are visible only from some seats, or only on foot.
3. **Manoeuvres that need talking:** Luigi's cliff-edge reverse is the model. More tight spots where the pedals and the wheel must be in sync (narrow ledges, three-point turns, slow-crawl rock gardens), which also fits the 31 Oct review's wish for more obstacles and fewer long straights.
