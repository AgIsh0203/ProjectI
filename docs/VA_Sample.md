# Squirrel Wheels: voice sample

Thanks for trying this! Below are a few sample lines, so you can get a feel for the characters and show what you can do with them.

## The game
*Squirrel Wheels* (working title) is a silly co-op driving game for 2–4 players. A crew of tiny squirrels, sick of plain acorns, sneaks onto a gourmet delivery truck to steal a crate of **golden acorns**. The truck drives off with them, crashes at a junkyard hundreds of kilometres from home, and the squirrels build a falling-apart **junk jeep** out of a toy jeep and trash. Now they have to drive the golden acorns home before the first snow, while the jeep breaks down and squirrels keep falling out.

**The squirrels never talk.** Everything you hear comes from a **CB radio** they salvaged from the crashed truck, crackling on the jeep's dashboard. Players hear you while they're panicking, so short, punchy lines that are easy to understand over chaos work best.

**You play everyone on the radio**, in your own voice; no old-lady voice needed. Play with pace, attitude and accent rather than pitch. Each character should be recognisable from a single line.

## The characters and lines

### 1. The Dispatcher
The gourmet acorn company's dispatcher, tracking the stolen cargo. Dry, deadpan and unimpressed by everything, very professional about very stupid things. She's the main voice of the story.

- "Unit Seven, this is Dispatch. Be advised: your cargo has left the truck. ...Your cargo is driving. Over."
- "All units, be on the lookout for a small jeep. Possibly a toy. Driven by... I'm being told squirrels. Over."
- "Unit Seven, the client is asking for an ETA on the golden acorns. I've told them 'rodent-dependent'. Over."
- *(the jeep goes off a cliff)* "...Noted."
- *(a checkpoint is reached)* "Logging their position. For the insurance claim."

### 2. The Beaver Foreman
Owns the excavator the squirrels take. Jittery and fast, panicky and protective of his machine.

- "No-no-no-no, don't touch the — that's my digger, that's a WORK digger — oh, they're touching it."
- "Okay, okay, if you're gonna smash the ranger station, at least use the BUCKET, not the cab! The CAB is where I eat!"

### 3. The Trucker
Phones in sightings. Big, warm, slow drawl, loves gossip on the radio.

- "Breaker breaker, I just got passed by a jeep with four squirrels in it. And one of 'em was steerin' with its whole body."
- "Dispatch, one of 'em just flew out the door. ...He's fine. He's runnin' after it. Good for him."

### 4. The Conspiracy Guy
Whispery and paranoid, absolutely sure he's right.

- "They're organised now. First the bird feeders, now the vehicles. Next? The *infrastructure*."
- "Don't say 'acorn' on an open channel. They're LISTENING."

### Bonus: the ending, played straight
The Dispatcher, quiet, almost warm for once:

- "Dispatch to all units. First snow's falling. ...Somewhere out there, a crate of golden acorns made it home. Stand down. Over."

## How to record
- Record each line 2–3 times: one deadpan, one bigger, and one panicked where it fits.
- Leave a second of silence before and after each take.
- Any decent mic is fine (a headset or phone in a quiet room works for a sample); any format.
- **Improvising is very welcome.** The best lines in games like this are often ad-libs. If a character suggests a better joke to you, record it.
