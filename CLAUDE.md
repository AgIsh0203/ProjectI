# ProjectI — "Squirrel Wheels" (working title)

20-day UE 5.7.1 co-op "friend slop" **prototype**: 2–4 tiny squirrels drive ONE human-sized, open-top, falling-apart car. Guiding principle: **viewer fun first, player fun second**, so the game spreads through streams and clips.

## Locked decisions (don't relitigate)
- **Players and camera:** 2-player minimum, no bots. Third-person chase camera, wider while seated. Arcade feel. Repair juggling is the heart of the game.
- **Score:** acorns delivered (they spill from the open bed) + time bonus.
- **Bad endings:** the deadline runs out, or a total wreck (3+ parts failed for 10 s).
- **Acorn in Mouth:** every 75–105 s, weighted toward the Wheel seat or a squirrel at a failing part. Muted for 20 s, can still hear, has an 8-ping chat wheel.
- **Code quality:** foundation-quality C++ for systems (car, parts, seats, sessions, voice). Throwaway Blueprints/data for content.
- **Online:** Steam OSS, listen server, AppID 480 in dev. Proximity voice via VOIPTalker.
- **Release:** free public itch demo. Steam only after a "go" signal (needs $100 fee + 2-week Coming Soon page).
- **Title:** a squirrel/wheels pun, decided before release. The C++ module stays `ProjectI`.
- **Assets:** Epic template content (the Chaos vehicle) is committed publicly. The user decided this.

## Code map (`Source/ProjectI/`)
- `Car/NBCar` — derives from `ATP_VehicleAdvOffroadCar` (Chaos).
  - Never possessed: the server feeds Chaos from `SetSeatInput`.
  - Physics replication mode is `PredictiveInterpolation`.
  - `SetRequiresControllerForInputs(!HasAuthority())`, so clients use Chaos's `ReplicatedState`. This fixed client wheel jitter.
  - Template auto-flip-reset is disabled (`FlipCheckMinDot = -2`). Our own flip-up replaces it:
    - A 0.25 s server timer (`CheckFlipped`) checks the car. If its up axis is below `FlipUpDot` (0.35) and it's slower than 200 cm/s for 1.5 s, it counts as flipped (replicated `bFlipped`).
    - Flipping ejects every rider, zeroes the inputs and closes the seats (`FindNearestFreeSeat` / `FindNextFreeSeat` return null, so respawns land beside the car).
    - It also enables `FlipSpot`: Push2, 2 users, on foot, Range 360, at the middle of the body.
    - Completing the push lifts the car 120 cm and sets it upright, keeping its yaw. If the car rolls back onto its wheels by itself (up axis > 0.8), the flipped state clears.
    - Dev console: `NBFlip` rolls the car onto its roof. The HUD shows a "CAR FLIPPED!" banner.
  - **Reset to the trail** (18 Oct, for a car down a cliff): any squirrel holds R (rebindable `ENBBinding::ResetCar`; gamepad View) for `ResetHoldSeconds` (1.5 s, counted locally in `ANBSquirrel::Tick`), then `Server_RequestCarReset` → `ANBCar::ResetToTrail` (BlueprintCallable for tests): back where the car last was on the trail (`LastOnTrailDistance`, recorded by `UpdateOffroad` while ≥ 3 wheels are down and most are on the trail) minus `ResetBackOff` (8 m), never the nearest trail anywhere (below a cliff that could be a later leg: a shortcut); `GetTransformAt`, 150 cm up; upright, zero velocity, flip cleared, crash detection reset; spills `AcornsLostOnReset` (3), then `ResetCooldown` (15 s, replicated `ResetReadyTime`). Squirrels on foot `ReturnToCar` (RespawnAtCar without the respawn penalty). Replicated `ResetCount` → "CAR RESET!  -3 acorns" on the HUD; `DrawCarReset` also shows the hold bar and, after 3 s slow and off the trail or flipped, "STUCK?  Hold R: back on the trail". Logs `NBReset: ...`.
  - **Teleports go through `TeleportCar`** (server, 23 Oct): the reset, the flip-up, `NBFlip`, `NBWarp` and the autopilot's unflip. It sets the transform, zeroes the velocities and sends the reliable `Multicast_Teleport`, which does the same on each client and removes the stale physics-replication target (`IPhysicsReplication::RemoveReplicatedTarget`). Predictive interpolation has no distance snap: a plain server teleport glided on clients for ~3 s (a 9 m reset was still 0.9 m off after 2.4 s), and without removing the target it was pulled back 2 m after the snap. Don't `SetActorTransform` the car directly.
  - Damage hooks: `SetEnginePowerScale`, `SetBrakePowerScale`, `SetWheelGripScale` (scale the base values cached in BeginPlay).
  - Steering fades with speed (18 Oct, the user: bends were too easy at top speed): `ApplyInputsToVehicle` (now every server tick) scales the Wheel seat's input from 1 at `SteerFadeStart` (500 cm/s) to `SteerAtSpeed` (0.4) at `SteerFadeFull` (1500), under "Car|Handling". PIE: full lock at 1300 cm/s turned the car 16° in 2 s (33° before).
  - **Wheels use `ESweepShape::Spherecast`** (`TP_VehicleAdvOffroadWheelFront/Rear` constructors). The template's `Shapecast` has no case of its own in UE 5.7's Chaos suspension trace and falls through to the raycast path, so the wheels were raycasting: the ray slipped between trail rocks and ford pebbles and the suspension never felt them (18 Oct). A random-kick "rubble" effect was tried first and reverted: it fired on the whole Rocks stretch, rocks or not, and felt unnatural.
  - Engine state: the server samples Chaos RPM and gear every tick into replicated `RepEngineRPM` (rounded to 10) / `RepGear`; clients smooth it (`GetEngineRPM`, `GetEngineRPMAlpha`, `GetEngineGear`).
  - **Gears seat** (G5, 18 Oct): the PassengerSeat has role `ENBSeatRole::Gears` (added after Rider in the enum). `GetSeat(Wheel|Pedals|Gears)`; `ClearSeatInput(role)` is what leaving a seat calls (zeroes the axis, and the Gears seat lets go of the handbrake).
    - Handbrake (any mode): `SetHandbrake` (replicated `bHandbrake`) → `SetHandbrakeInput(bRunLocked || bHandbrake)`, the template's rear-wheel handbrake. A flip clears it.
    - Manual gears: `SetManualGears` (replicated `bManualGears`; the game mode calls it) switches Chaos with `SetUseAutomaticGears`. The Gears seat's axis shifts once per push past ±0.5 (`GearStick` edge), `ManualGear` 1..`GetNumForwardGears()` (5). `HoldManualGear` (server tick) keeps Chaos in that gear unless braking: Chaos's reverse-as-brake still drops to reverse and back to 1st, and the car returns to `ManualGear`. Logs `NBGears: ...`.
    - Over-rev wear (manual only, in `UpdateWear`): from `OverRevStart` (0.65 of the rev range, ~4900 rpm) up to `OverRevWearRate` (0.25/s) at the redline, x `TopGearOverRevScale` (2) in 5th, where the HUD says "EASE OFF! OVERHEATING" instead of "SHIFT UP!".
    - Gearbox and power (18 Oct, set on BP_OffroadCar_Pawn by `Tools/Car/SetGearbox.py`; edit its numbers and rerun it, `SAVE = False` to try values in PIE first): ratios 16.4 / 10.2 / 7.5 / 5.9 / 4.85 (final 2.81), max torque 80 Nm (template 750), automatic up at 4700 rpm, down at 2600. The torque curve (`FC_OffroadCar_Torque`) is 0 from 5000 rpm, so each gear's top is set by its ratio, and the torque sets how fast you get there. The user's aim (18 Oct): most of the trail in 3rd–4th, 2nd for slow parts and climbs, 5th only after 10+ s flat out on a straight. Automatic from a standstill (PIE, 85 Nm): 2nd at 1.8 s, 3rd 3.4 s, 4th 7.2 s, 5th 9.9 s (~1500 cm/s); top ~1650 after a long straight. 18 % climbs go in 1st/2nd (~500–780 cm/s). With 80 Nm the car no longer drives over trail rocks on a climb: the autopilot (which aims at the centreline) gets stuck on PINE PASS and ROCKY ROAD rubble, a driver steers round it. History in the script.
  - Sound/VFX slots (all empty until assets are assigned in BP_OffroadCar_Pawn, category "Car|Feedback"): `EngineSound` on the `EngineAudio` component (float params RPM, Throttle, EngineFailed; pitch from RPM for a plain loop), `CrashFeedback` (velocity change ≥ 500 cm/s per 0.1 s, intensity up to 1600), `SpillFeedback`, `FlippedFeedback`, `RightedFeedback`, `HornFeedback`. Played through the unreliable `Multicast_Feedback`.
  - Horn: a seated squirrel's `Server_Honk` → `Honk()` (server, `HornCooldown` 0.25 s) → `Multicast_Feedback(Horn)`, which also stamps the local `GetLastHornTime()` for the HUD's "HONK!" bubble.
  - Crash camera punch: the Crash multicast calls `ShakeLocalCameras`, which punches each local squirrel's camera (`ANBSquirrel::AddCameraPunch`): full for squirrels attached to the car, fading to 0 at `CrashShakeRadius` (25 m) on foot. No time dilation: it's global.
- `Car/NBCarBody` — `UNBCarBody` (a data asset): a car's look and layout on the shared template skeleton (A2, 26 Oct). `BodyMesh` (replaces `Chassis`), `WheelMesh` (the 4 tires), `DoorMesh` (replaces the greybox `DoorPanel`; origin = hinge), `EngineMesh` + `EngineTransform` (the `EngineMesh` component), `Locations` / `Ranges` by component name (seats, parts, `DoorHinge`, `EngineAudio`, ...), `AcornPileCorner`. `ANBCar::CarBody` (EditDefaultsOnly) is applied by `ApplyBody` in `OnConstruction`, so every machine builds it from the class defaults; null = the buggy. `GetAcornPileCorner` feeds the pile and the spills. The door flaps outward on its hinge's side (`UNBDoorPart` flips `OpenYaw` for a left-side hinge).
  - **The junk jeep:** `BP_JunkJeep` (child of BP_OffroadCar_Pawn in the always-cooked OffroadCar folder, `CarBody` = `/Game/ThirdParty/PolyPizza/JunkJeep/DA_JunkJeepBody`). The game mode spawns it (`FClassFinder`, falling back to the buggy); `?Car=Buggy` in the URL spawns `BuggyClass`. Right-hand drive, so the passenger (Gears) seat and the cardboard door are on the left (-y). Jobs: HoodSeat → battery engine, DeckSeat (back right) → sponge brake, PassengerSeat → door.
  - Pipeline: `Tools/Blender/build_junkjeep.py -- SourceArt/JunkJeep/junkjeep_layout.json SourceArt/JunkJeep` (headless Blender) fits the jeep onto the template wheel bones (wheelbase scale, width x1.3), drops its spare tyre, places the trash props from the layout (Unreal coordinates), writes `SM_JunkJeep_Body/Door/Engine/Wheel.fbx` and `preview_*.png` with the seats as boxes. Then `Tools/Car/MakeJunkJeep.py` (editor Python) imports them, gives the body one box round the tub (UE 5.7's Interchange FBX import drops `UCX_` boxes, so the script sets `agg_geom`) and fills the data asset and the Blueprint. Seats/parts live in `MakeJunkJeep.py`'s `LOCATIONS` (keep in step with the layout's `seats`). The editor's game mode CDO only finds a new BP after an editor restart.
  - Materials: `MakeJunkJeep.py` moves every slot off the FBX import's legacy Phong instances (near-black colours, black in shade) onto `MI_JunkJeep_<slot>` instances of `/Engine/BasicShapes/BasicShapeMaterial`, colour lifted by a square root; the windscreen slot gets the translucent `M_JunkJeepGlass`.
  - The template's `Chassis` collision is welded into the car, so the body mesh's simple collision is part of how the car hits things: keep a box (or hull) on any new body.
- `Car/NBSeatComponent` — replicated seat (Wheel / Pedals / Rider / Gears); the squirrel attaches to it, so its location is the capsule centre.
  - The car has 5: WheelSeat (perched on the steering wheel), PedalSeat (footwell), HoodSeat (front hood), PassengerSeat (the Gears seat), DeckSeat (engine deck on the buggy, the back on the jeep). The constructor's positions are the buggy's (measured from `SM_Offroad_Body` vertices; its comment lists the landmarks); a car body moves them.
  - Hop order is the car's `Seats` array (filled in BeginPlay).
- `Player/NBSquirrel` — character. The game mode spawns its Blueprint child `/Game/Blueprints/BP_Squirrel` (`FClassFinder`, falling back to the C++ class); assign squirrel assets there. `/Game/Blueprints` is always cooked.
  - Enhanced Input is built in code (no input assets; `BuildInputAssets` makes the actions, `MapKeys` maps them and reruns when `UNBControlSettings::GetBindingsRevision` changes): WASD, mouse look, Space = jump, E = interact, LMB = action, H = horn (seated only), R held = reset the car to the trail, Left Shift held = handbrake (Gears seat only; `Server_SetHandbrake`), V held = talk with push to talk on, keys 1–8 = pings, Esc or Tab = lobby menu (Tab because Esc stops Play In Editor; in PIE the HUD names Tab). Gamepad (defaults, rebindable since 25 Oct: `UNBControlSettings::GetPadKey`): A jump, X interact, B action, Y horn, RB handbrake, View reset, L3 held talk (push to talk); fixed: Start menu, the sticks; pings on the D-pad (left / right / up / down = pings 1–4; with LB held 5–8: `PadPingActions`, `PadPingPressed` checks LB via `IsInputKeyDown`; #14, 20 Oct), and the HUD's muted banner and ping legend name them when `bUsingGamepad`. On the Gears seat W/S (stick up/down) go to the server as the seat axis; with automatic gears they only raise a seat hint.
  - Sound/VFX slots ("Squirrel|Feedback"): `ScreamFeedback` (played on the flying body when the ragdoll starts, on every machine; silent for a late joiner's old fall), `LandFeedback` (the ragdoll body's `OnBodyHit`, impact speed = impulse / mass from `LandMinSpeed` 250 to `LandMaxSpeed` 1500, `LandCooldown` 0.25 s), `GrabFeedback` (the unreliable `Multicast_Grabbed` on an action press, at most every 1.5 s so mashing doesn't machine-gun it).
  - Camera punch: `AddCameraPunch(0..1)` jitters `FollowCamera` by up to `CameraPunchDistance` (35 cm) and `CameraPunchRoll` (4°), squared, decaying at `CameraPunchDecay` per second. Local only.
  - Seated camera: arm `SeatedArmLength` 420, pivot `SeatedCameraHeight` 110 (650 / 150 until G7, 18 Oct), the auto-follow eases to `SeatedFollowPitch` -12°. On foot, E takes the nearest free seat. Seated, E hops to the next free seat (`HopToNextSeat`) and Space jumps out on the seat's side of the car.
  - Fallen-squirrel cam (#12, 20 Oct; `UpdateFallenCamera`, local, not riding): while tumbling, or on foot beyond `FallenCamStartDistance` (12 m) from the car during Driving only (the lobby wanders THE PARK), the control rotation eases (`FallenFollowSpeed`) to look past the squirrel at the car (pitch towards it + `FallenPitchOffset` -12°, clamped -40..15; not within 4 m), so W runs back to it; look input holds it off for `FallenFollowDelay` 1.5 s. The arm stretches from `OnFootArmLength` to `FallenArmLength` (750) between 12 and `FallenCamFullDistance` (40 m), at least `FallenTumbleStretch` (0.4) while tumbling. Under "Camera|Fallen".
  - Action press and release go to the server (`Server_ActionPressed` carries the client's server-time estimate), which uses `UNBInteractableComponent::FindBestFor`.
  - Seated: movement and collision off, the move axis goes to the server via `Server_SetSeatInput`, and the camera boom has collision off and is pulled back. Above 150 cm/s, after 1 s without look input, the camera swings behind the car (`SeatedFollowDelay`, `SeatedFollowSpeed`, `SeatedFollowMinSpeed`). On the Pedals seat the tail sticks up as a flag (`PedalTailTransform`) so the low footwell squirrel shows over the dash.
  - Each player gets a unique, replicated fur color (`ColorIndex` → `FurColors`, applied via the BasicShapeMaterial `Color` param to the body, tail, head and ears; `SetPartColor` tints the rest in BeginPlay).

While tumbling, every entry of `RideAlongParts` (tail, belly, head root) attaches to the body ball, then returns to its standing pose.
  - Model (rigged 18 Oct): `ModelMesh` (a skeletal mesh) shows `/Game/ThirdParty/PolyPizza/squirrel_n/Rigged/SK_Squirrel` (CC-BY, 1 m tall, origin between the feet) at `ModelMeshTransform` (z -22, scale 0.48), with `/Game/Blueprints/M_SquirrelFur` (BasicShapeMaterial can't skin; same `Color` parameter) tinted with the fur colour. Note `GetMesh()` is ACharacter's own, empty mesh: find the model by the name `ModelMesh`.

Sequences: `A_Squirrel_<Name>` next to the mesh, found in the constructor.

    - Pipeline: `Tools/Blender/` (README there) builds the rig and animations from `SourceArt/Squirrel/<model>.rig.json` in headless Blender (`C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe`), checks paw/tail clipping into the head, renders contact sheets, and `import_squirrel.py` (editor Python) re-imports: it deletes the Rigged folder first, because UE 5.7's Interchange FBX import skips the animations when the mesh already exists.
  - Charm (`UpdateCharm`, every machine but a dedicated server, cosmetic only): `SampleCharmAcceleration` takes the actor's smoothed acceleration in its own frame (clamped, reset on a > 3 m jump). It drives a tail spring that swings the tail about its root end, on top of `TailBaseTransform` (the curl or the pedal flag), plus an idle sway. It also drives googly pupils that sag under gravity and bounce off the eye's rim. Tuning is under "Squirrel|Charm".
  - Falling: `Eject(velocity, reason)` / `EjectFromCar(reason)` (the car's velocity plus `EjectKick` out of the seat's side and up). The `ENBEjectReason` (Door, Flipped, Bailed, River, Other) replicates in `FNBRagdollState`; each machine stamps `GetLastEjectReason` / `GetLastEjectTime` when the ragdoll starts, and the HUD says why for 3 s (friend test 2).
    - The "ragdoll" detaches `BodyVisual` (the tail rides on it) as a physics ball. Each machine simulates it from the replicated `FNBRagdollState` (start + velocity), and the capsule follows it in Tick.
    - After `RagdollSeconds` (2.5) the server gets the squirrel up where its body landed (`SnapTo` + `Client_SnapTo`, since the owning client runs its own movement).

  - Dev console: `NBWarp <n|name>` teleports the car to a section start (riders come along, anyone on foot respawns at the car). `NBAuto [cm/s]` (or `?Autopilot=<cm/s>` in the URL, which also starts the run with one squirrel): `ANBRunGameMode::DevAutopilot` drives the car down the centreline with no random failures (starting the run if it's waiting), backs out when stuck, and logs each section (`NBAuto: ...`: time, speeds, slowest climb, max tilt, acorns lost, air points, stuck count) and `NBAuto: run over`. **Before a scripted drive in PIE, seat the local squirrel in a rider seat** (`enter_seat` on PassengerSeat): the user watches. The editor's Python keeps globals between scripts, so don't rely on `globals().get` defaults.
- `Car/NBAcorn` + cargo on `NBCar`: 40 acorns, drawn with the CC-BY acorn model (`SM_Acorn`, 1 m, so the 0.14 scale makes 14 cm; a spilled acorn keeps a hidden sphere for physics). `Acorns` is `ReplicatedUsing=OnRep_Acorns`, which (like the server's `SpillAcorns`) calls `RefreshAcornPile`: the `AcornPile` ISM on the engine deck shows exactly `Acorns` spheres, from `AcornPileSlots` (layers of 5 x 4, fixed seed, bottom first, so spills empty it from the top). A velocity change > 350 cm/s per 0.1 s spills acorns as server-spawned, replicated physics actors (20 s lifespan); a flip dumps 25 %.
- **Acorn in Mouth + chat wheel:** `Game/NBRunGameMode::GiveAcornInMouth` mutes one squirrel for 20 s every 75-105 s while driving (weights: Wheel seat +2, next to a failed part +2; skipped in the last 20 s). State lives on `ANBPlayerState` (`MuteEndTime`, last ping). The local `ANBSquirrel` calls `ToggleSpeaking(false/true)` to cut the open mic. Keys 1-8 send a ping, muted or not (1 s cooldown, shown 4 s left of screen); the HUD shows banners and a legend while muted.
- **Proximity voice:** every `ANBSquirrel` has a `UNBVoiceTalker` (`Audio/`, a `UVOIPTalker` that hands each incoming voice's audio component to `UNBAudioSettings`) registered with its PlayerState (BeginPlay / PossessedBy / OnRep_PlayerState). Spatialised on the squirrel, full volume within 800 cm, fading to silence over 2500 cm more (`VoiceFullRadius`, `VoiceFalloff`). The local squirrel sets `SetAudioListenerAttenuationOverride` to itself, so distances are measured from the squirrel, not the camera metres behind it (friend test 1: voices too quiet).
  - **Push to talk** (25 Oct, a per-player choice; default off = open mic): `UNBAudioSettings::IsPushToTalk` (config). `ANBSquirrel::UpdateVoiceMute` (local, every tick) decides whether the mic sends: never during Acorn in Mouth; with push to talk only while `ENBBinding::PushToTalk` (V) or its gamepad button (L3) is down, polled with `IsInputKeyDown` (opening the menu flushes keys, so it can't stick on). It calls `ToggleSpeaking` only on a change, and again for a new controller, because the engine turns voice on at login (`ClientEnableNetworkVoice`). The HUD shows MIC ON while sending with push to talk, and the waiting/countdown hint names the key. `-game -nosteam` check: login starts networked voice, the next frame stops it.
- `Audio/NBAudioSettings` — game-instance subsystem, `config=GameUserSettings`: game volume (`SetTransientPrimaryVolume`, default 0.7), voice volume (each voice component's multiplier is `VoiceVolume / GameVolume`, capped at 4, so the game slider doesn't turn voices down; default 1.5), mic boost (`voice.MicInputGain`, default 1.5) and the output device (`UAudioMixerBlueprintLibrary::SwapAudioOutputDevice`). The mic is Windows' default input: UE's Steam voice opens `CreateVoiceCapture("")` and only protected engine code can switch it. Edited on the lobby menu's Options page, Sound tab.
- `Game/NBSessionSubsystem` — game-instance subsystem for Steam lobbies, driven by the lobby menu.
  - "Host a game" (or `NBHost`) creates a lobby tagged `NBGAME=SquirrelWheels` (so searches on AppID 480 only find ours), then reopens L_TestTrack with `?listen`.
  - "Join a game" (or `NBJoin`) finds a lobby and joins it. "Leave the game" destroys the session and reopens the map offline. A network failure (e.g. the host left) sets the menu's status line. An accepted Steam invite, or "Join Game" from the friends list, joins automatically.
  - Each step destroys a stale session first. The HUD's top-left line shows OFFLINE / HOST / CONNECTED, the online subsystem and the session status.
- `TP_VehicleAdv/` — imported C++ Vehicle template, compiled inside the ProjectI module. Its duplicate `IMPLEMENT_PRIMARY_GAME_MODULE` was removed; `Build.cs` adds its folders to the include path.
- Maps: `/Game/Maps/L_Route` (the baked route; default, startup and hosting map) and `/Game/Maps/L_TestTrack` (greybox floor, ramps, bumps, the four interact test pads; the runtime-built route still works there).

## Config gotchas (already applied; keep them)
- **Physics substepping ON** (≤8.3 ms, 12 max, MaxPhysicsDeltaTime 0.1). Without it, Chaos tires popped up to ~16 cm at uneven frame rates.
- **Physics Prediction OFF.** Turning it on stops the unpossessed Chaos car from moving: it switches vehicles to the network-prediction input path.
- **The net driver is SteamSockets** (plugin enabled, `bUseSteamNetworking`, relays on). Without Steam (e.g. PIE) it falls back to IpNetDriver.
  - UE 5.7 no longer has `OnlineSubsystemSteam.SteamNetDriver`; the old config silently fell back to IP.
  - Voice is open mic by default (`bRequiresPushToTalk=false`); each player can switch to push to talk in Options (above), done in our code, not this flag.
- **Packaging:** `/Game/VehicleTemplate/Blueprints/OffroadCar`, `/Game/Blueprints` (BP_Squirrel), `/Game/ThirdParty` (the HUD font) and `/Engine/BasicShapes` are always cooked, because only C++ constructors reference them. Anything new that only C++ references (a HUD font, an imported mesh) needs the same treatment; `docs/Assets.md` has the folder plan.
  - Package with: `RunUAT BuildCookRun -platform=Win64 -clientconfig=Development -build -cook -stage -pak -iostore -nodebuginfo -archive -archivedirectory=...`
  - Linking the monolithic `ProjectI.exe` with full debug info needs ~16 GB of RAM. With Firefox or a game open, UBA kills the link at its memory threshold and retries for ~20 min; close big apps before packaging. (`DebugInfoLineTablesOnly` in the Target.cs does nothing with MSVC; `DebugInfo = DebugInfoMode.ProjectOnly` would fix it at the cost of an engine recompile.)
  - **Linux** (20 Oct): the same command with `-platform=Linux`, cross-compiled with the `v26_clang-20.1.8-rockylinux8` toolchain (`LINUX_MULTIARCH_ROOT`; matches `Engine/Config/Linux/Linux_SDK.json`). Clang builds with `-Werror -Wshadow`, which MSVC doesn't: a local named like a member (e.g. `Ground` in `ANBRoute`) fails only the Linux build. The first Linux cook took ~18 min (global Vulkan SM6 shaders; a few TSR permutations take 30–115 s each). Linux targets `SF_VULKAN_SM6` only (`DefaultEngine.ini`, from the template). After staging, move `Engine/Binaries/ThirdParty/Vulkan/Linux/*VkLayer*` (237 MB of Vulkan debug layers, loaded only with Vulkan debugging on) out of the build, and pack it as `.tar.gz` with the binary at mode 755 (a zip made on Windows loses the exec bits).
  - **Editor-only plugins carry `"TargetAllowList": ["Editor"]`** in `ProjectI.uproject` (23 Oct): MCPUnreal, RemoteControl, Python, EditorScriptingUtilities, and the default-on MetaHumanSDK, Bridge, Fab, GameplayCameras, CameraShakePreviewer, UVEditor, GLTFExporter, NFORDenoise, ResonanceAudio. A plugin enabled for the game drags its dependencies into the monolithic exe even when its own modules are editor-only (MCPUnreal pulled in PCG and GameplayAbilities; MetaHumanSDK pulled in RigLogic; UVEditor pulled in MeshModelingToolset). Check a new plugin with `Build.bat ProjectI Win64 Development -Project=... -Mode=JsonExport -OutputFile=x.json` (the module list, no compile). Keep ControlRig/RigVM: the template car's `Offroad_CtrlRig` uses them.
  - `-nodebuginfo` keeps the 2.3 GB PDB out of the build. Copy the PDB from `Binaries/Win64` to `ProjectI_Builds\<date>\Symbols\` so crash dumps can still be read.
  - The size budget and the steps to meet it are in `docs/Roadmap.md` (Package size). Write each build's zip size in the playtest notes.
- **No Nanite on any mesh** (26 Oct: 27 imported meshes had it on, and with SM5 the editor kept popping "Missing Project Settings: SM6 is required to use Nanite assets"). Turn it off on new imports.
- **Committed `.uasset` files check out read-only** (LFS `lockable` in `.gitattributes`), so the editor's saves fail with "Cannot remove ... as it is read only". Clear the flag on the files you change (`(Get-Item f).IsReadOnly = $false`), or `git lfs lock` them.
- **Shader Model 5 only** (23 Oct, Roadmap size phase D): `D3D12TargetedShaderFormats` and Linux `TargetedRHIs` are SM5 only (`!...=ClearArray`). SM6 compiled ~124 MB of global shaders for MegaLights, Nanite, Lumen and virtual shadow maps even with all of them off. The editor still runs at SM6 (the cook commandlet logged SM6), so check the look in a build. Nanite needs SM6: don't enable it without bringing SM6 back.
- **Motion blur OFF** (`r.DefaultFeature.MotionBlur=False`): it smeared the spinning tires into noise.
- **Software VRS / contrast-adaptive shading OFF** (`Config/Windows/WindowsEngine.ini`: `r.VRS.EnableSoftware=0`, `r.VRS.ContrastAdaptiveShading=0`). UE 5.7's BaseWindowsEngine.ini turns them on. On 18 Oct the editor hung the GPU twice (DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG, RX 7900 XTX, Adrenalin 26.8.1), both times on the async compute queue inside `PrepareImageBasedVRS → ContrastAdaptiveShading`. For a GPU hang, read the log's "Active GPU breadcrumbs" for every pipeline, not only Graphics: the graphics queue just shows whatever was waiting.
- **Water collision channel** (`ECC_GameTraceChannel1` "Water", default response Ignore, and the "Water" profile; `NBCollision::Water` in `NBRoute.h`): river water uses it so everything passes through, and `ANBCar` sets its wheel traces to ignore it too, and PhysicsBody as well, so knocked cones and spilled acorns get shoved rather than climbed. Before 18 Oct the baked water blocked and the car drove on top of the river.
- **Bake collision by profile name only.** A bare `set_collision_enabled` from Python shows in the editor but doesn't reach PIE or the game, and setting an actor's tags re-runs its construction and resets collision: BakeRoute.py tags first, then sets a profile (`BlockAll`, `NoCollision`, `Water`).
- **PIE runs 2 players as a listen server**, from `Config/DefaultEditorPerProjectUserSettings.ini`. The per-user `Saved/` ini can override it.
